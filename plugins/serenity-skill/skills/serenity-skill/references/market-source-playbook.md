# Market Source Playbook

Use when market-specific evidence matters. US and A-share receive full treatment; other markets get methodology-level guidance only.

## US

**Primary sources:** SEC 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, S-1, S-3, Form 4; earnings transcripts and IR presentations; company press releases; customer/supplier filings; patents, standards, conference papers, trade publications.

**Key checks:** shelf/ATM/converts/SBC/insider selling; customer concentration (Mag7 presence as demand signal, single-customer as risk); backlog and revenue mix; GAAP gross margin (not non-GAAP); SI and options volatility; sell-side estimate gap; signed-contract ARR vs. market-cap mismatch; financing quality spectrum (strategic investment > convertibles > colo > ATM > heavy debt); qualification cycle vs. TTM revenue for pre-ramp names.

## A-Shares

**Primary sources:** 年报、半年报、季报、临时公告; 交易所问询函/监管函; 互动易/上证e互动; 招投标/中标公告/客户验厂认证; 环评/能评/地方项目备案/土地产线设备进度; 专利/标准/行业协会数据; 海关数据/上下游上市公司交叉验证.

**Key checks:** 应收/存货/合同负债/经营现金流; 毛利率/产能利用率/在建工程转固; 关联交易/资产注入/客户真实性; 定增/可转债/股权质押/商誉; 政策补贴和订单商业性; 主题炒作后估值压力; 国产替代/自主可控政策落地节奏; 产能扩张的环评/能评/土地硬证据.

## Other Markets (Methodology Only)

The bottleneck logic transfers to Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, Korea, and Europe. Apply the same 9-step workflow and use `references/evidence-ladder.md` to grade sources. For market-specific paths, consult: HKEX (HK), MOPS (TW), TDnet (JP), DART (KR), local exchange filings (EU).
