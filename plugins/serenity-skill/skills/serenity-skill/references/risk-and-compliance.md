# Risk and Research Boundary

## Core Boundary

Research support only: ranked priorities, evidence and uncertainty, value-chain reasoning, risk/downside checks, next verification actions. The user makes trading decisions.

## High-Risk Situations

Extra caution when: micro-cap/thinly traded; social-media-driven; single unnamed customer; new financing/convertibles/placements/heavy dilution; weak cash flow with large capex; valuation assumes perfect execution; policy/export controls/sanctions/military end-use/local approvals drive thesis.

## Serenity-Specific Caveats

When referencing `ticker-theses.md`:
- **Self-reported, unverified returns.** YTD figures are screenshots only.
- **Calibration:** ~61% 30-day directional accuracy, ~41% strict hits. Mature photonics/CPO bottleneck theses validate better (~75–85%).
- **Survivorship bias.** Public feed highlights winners; losers get less airtime.
- **High-volatility small-caps.** Names move 20%+ daily; his sizing/margin not appropriate to copy.
- **Theses decay and reverse.** Multiple tickers changed tier/direction within months (IREN, CRWV, SNAP, HOOD, POET). Always re-confirm.
- **Lens, not signal feed.** This is a tool to ask better questions. NOT auto-trading, NOT financial advice.
- **No-position posts carry lower weight.** Exploratory or "for fun" comments are process examples, not conviction calls.

## Allowed vs. Avoid

**Allowed:** "I would prioritize this layer first." "This ranks higher because it sits closer to the real expansion constraint." "This is still only a lead because..." "I would downgrade it if..."

**Avoid:** guaranteed return language; direct order instructions; coordinated buying; rumor-based recommendations; material non-public information; invented prices/filings/customers; treating self-reported returns as proof; copying position sizing/margin use.

## Safety Checks (Pre-Ranking)

1. latest filings and financing risk
2. liquidity and valuation
3. revenue quality, inventory, receivables, cash flow
4. customer evidence and substitution routes
5. governance and related-party risk
6. policy and geopolitical exposure
7. what fact would weaken the idea
8. (US covered names) current stance/conviction tier; owned vs. exploratory; sector calibration band
