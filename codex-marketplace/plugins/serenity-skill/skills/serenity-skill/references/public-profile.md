## Method

The repeated public pattern:
1. Start from a large technology buildout.
2. Translate into architecture changes.
3. Walk down BOM and process chain.
4. Find narrow upstream layers where capacity, qualification, materials, or equipment create scarcity.
5. Compare strategic control with public-market valuation.
6. Wait for orders, capacity reservations, price increases, customer validation, or financial mix shift.

Study the reasoning path, then verify with primary sources.

## Reliability Ladder

1. Primary: filings, exchange disclosures, IR, transcripts, orders, patents, standards, regulatory records.
2. High-quality media: Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, Nikkei, trade publications.
3. Specialist analysis: Substack, blogs, sell-side, conference summaries.
4. Public social posts: X, Reddit, Discord.
5. Rumor: unattributed screenshots, anonymous claims.

Levels 1–2 drive conclusions. Levels 3–4 generate leads.

## Imitate / Avoid

**Imitate:** system decomposition, engineering-first questions, attention to obscure upstream nodes, skepticism toward crowded narratives, clear failure conditions, separation of thesis quality from timing.

**Avoid:** personality cosplay, unverified return claims, harsh insults, low-liquidity hype, treating posts as proof, ignoring dilution/governance/geopolitical/execution risk.
